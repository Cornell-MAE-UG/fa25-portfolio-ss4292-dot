This project investigates the idea of *fluid-mechanical dissection* by dissecting a lawnmower to look into the fluid mechanics that underlie its construction and functioning. The project links theoretical fluid-mechanical principles to practical engineering applications by deconstructing the device, identifying its essential parts, and analyzing their functioning. The method places a strong emphasis on practical investigation, experimentation, and learning how complex systems function through the interplay of its constituent parts.
