**Success Criteria:**


A spinning mechanism that effectively removes objects with ample power and stability
- The brush should be able to remove 80% of sticky notes cut into strips of 2cm after 20 revolutions when rotating at its operational speed. This is a high priority.
- A brush mechanism which is securely suspended by two arms. The mechanism should be able to carry out 50 revolutions with any deformations below 1cm.
- The brushes should be securely fit in the shaft and should not come loose after active use. We should feel no noticeable change in how snug the brush fits into the slot after 50 revolutions of the brush actively brushing on a surface.
- The brushes should be easily and quickly replaceable: we should be able to remove and replace an individual brush in under 30 seconds without excessive effort.
